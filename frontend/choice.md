# Selected Design Philosophy: Idea 2 - Swiss Minimalist Mirror (The Cold Diagnostic)

We commit fully to **Idea 2: Swiss Minimalist Mirror (The Cold Diagnostic)**. 

### Why This Fits Perfectly:
The "Incoherence Engine" is not a standard chat app; it is a **diagnostic mirror** designed to expose the hollow, over-coherent, and sycophantic nature of AI. A clinical, high-end Swiss Minimalist aesthetic perfectly embodies this "cold truth." It strips away the cozy, rounded-corner, purple-gradient friendliness of consumer AI tools, replacing it with an objective, razor-sharp editorial interface.

### Key Design Commitments:
1. **Clinical Dark Palette**: Deepest obsidian black background, pure stark paper-white for user inputs, muted warm gold for medium warning annotations, and blood crimson for severe failure modes.
2. **Precision Layout**: An off-center, asymmetric layout. A clean left-side panel for settings (OpenRouter key, Model assignment: 1 primary + up to 3 analyzers), a wide central column for the live Diagnostic Mirror chat, and an elegant right-side collapsible panel for the Corpus References.
3. **Typography of Contrast**: Elegant serif display headers (e.g., *Cinzel* or *Playfair Display*) contrasting with clean, modern sans-serif body text (*Geist Sans*) and monospaced diagnostic metadata (*Geist Mono*).
4. **Analog Telemetry**: A custom, tactile J-Ratio (Liberation Ratio) meter rendered using solid unicode block characters (`███░░`) with an analog-feeling precision indicator (`J = ΔC / E`).
5. **No AI Slop**: Absolutely no purple gradients, no generic Inter-only typography, and no friendly consumer-app rounded corners. Everything is sharp, precise, and editorial.
